<h2>🚨 Top 5 Agency Red Flags in 2026</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Guaranteed Revenue or #1 Position Promises:</strong> In competitive real-time auction environments, no legitimate practitioner can guarantee static outcomes. Agencies that guarantee results often bid on your existing brand keywords to fake performance.</li>
  <li><strong>Holding Account Ownership Hostage:</strong> Never allow an agency to run your campaigns inside their own private account. If you part ways, years of pixel data, conversion history, and negative keyword architecture are lost. <em>Rule: All assets must remain 100% company-owned.</em></li>
  <li><strong>The Junior Handoff (Bait-and-Switch):</strong> The agency founder or senior partner pitches your business, but two weeks later your account is assigned to an overburdened junior coordinator managing 15+ accounts.</li>
  <li><strong>Percentage-of-Spend Model (15% - 20% Commission):</strong> If your agency earns more simply because you spend more, their incentive is to inflate ad spend, not optimize profit margin. A transparent flat-fee model eliminates this conflict of interest.</li>
  <li><strong>Lack of Server-Side Measurement:</strong> With Safari ITP, Firefox tracking protection, and ad blockers, client-side browser pixels lose 20%-35% of conversion events. An agency unable to implement Server-Side GTM and Meta CAPI is running blind.</li>
</ol>

<h2>📝 9 Critical Questions to Ask Before Signing</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Is ad spend billed directly to our corporate credit card?</strong> (Must be yes).</li>
  <li><strong>Who specifically will manage our account on a weekly basis?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>How do you handle negative keywords in Performance Max and Demand Gen?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Do you implement Server-Side GTM and Meta CAPI in-house?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>If we end the contract, do we retain all conversion tags, audiences, and campaign history?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>What KPIs are tracked in regular reporting?</strong> (Revenue, ROAS, CPA, and blended MER vs vanity metrics).</li>
  <li><strong>If our budget scales 3x, will your management fee also scale 3x?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Can you provide verified platform proof in our industry?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Are there lock-in termination penalties?</strong></li>
</ol>
