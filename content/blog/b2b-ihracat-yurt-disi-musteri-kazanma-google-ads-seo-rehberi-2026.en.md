<h2>🌍 Why B2B Exporters Must Shift from Trade Shows to Search &amp; AI Intent</h2>
<p>While a single 4-day international trade fair booth costs $15,000 to $40,000+, modern procurement directors in London, New York, Rotterdam, and Dubai research new OEM/ODM suppliers year-round on Google Search and AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). To capture these buyers without wasting budget on retail consumers, exporters must implement strict B2B pre-qualification:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Target High-Intent B2B Modifiers:</strong> Bid on <code>manufacturer</code>, <code>wholesale supplier</code>, <code>OEM factory</code>, <code>private label producer</code>, and <code>bulk distributor</code> rather than generic consumer product terms.</li>
  <li><strong>Include MOQ Qualifiers in Ad Copy:</strong> State minimum order quantities (e.g., <em>"MOQ 500 Units | B2B Wholesale Only"</em>) directly in RSA headlines to filter out single-item shoppers before they click.</li>
  <li><strong>Build High-Trust RFQ Landing Pages:</strong> Showcase ISO/CE/FDA certifications, production capacity, lead times, FOB/CIF/DDP shipping terms, and a frictionless written RFQ form backed by a <strong>24-hour guaranteed response SLA</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<h2>❓ Frequently Asked Questions (B2B Export Marketing &amp; International Google Ads)</h2>

<p><strong>What is the minimum monthly ad budget for B2B export Google Ads campaigns?</strong><br>
Because international B2B auctions in the UK, US, and EU operate in USD/EUR/GBP, we recommend a minimum monthly media budget of 25,000 TL to 50,000 TL+ ($1,000 to $2,500+) to gather statistically significant conversion data and train Smart Bidding algorithms.</p>

<p><strong>How do you prevent retail consumers from clicking B2B export ads?</strong><br>
We combine B2B-only keyword match types (<code>wholesale</code>, <code>manufacturer</code>, <code>bulk supplier</code>), negative keyword lists of 500+ retail/job terms, and explicit MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) qualifiers inside the ad headlines and multi-step RFQ forms.</p>

<p><strong>Is Google Consent Mode v2 required when advertising to European buyers?</strong><br>
Yes. Any website running Google Ads or GA4 targeting visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK must implement Google Consent Mode v2 to preserve conversion tracking and remarketing audiences.</p>
