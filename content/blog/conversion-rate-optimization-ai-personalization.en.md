<h2>🏢 What Does a Professional CRO Agency Do?</h2>
<p>A specialized CRO agency audits your user journeys, heatmaps, multi-step lead forms, and checkout funnels to eliminate cognitive and technical friction:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>GA4 &amp; GTM Micro-Funnel Diagnostics:</strong> Pinpoints the exact form field, checkout step, or device segment where prospective buyers abandon the journey.</li>
  <li><strong>Dynamic Message Matching:</strong> Aligns Google Ads search query intent directly with landing page H1 headlines and value propositions to boost both Quality Score (10/10) and conversion rates.</li>
  <li><strong>Frictionless B2B Lead Wizards:</strong> Replaces intimidating 10-field forms and mandatory phone-call demands with streamlined email/form qualification flows backed by a <strong>24-hour written response guarantee</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<h2>❓ Frequently Asked Questions (CRO Agency &amp; Conversion Optimization)</h2>

<p><strong>What does a CRO agency do?</strong><br>
A CRO agency analyzes user behavior through GA4 custom funnels, heatmaps, and session recordings, then runs structured A/B and multivariate tests on landing pages, forms, and checkouts to turn a higher percentage of existing traffic into revenue.</p>

<p><strong>How fast does Conversion Rate Optimization show results?</strong><br>
Technical quick wins—such as page speed (LCP) optimization, checkout friction removal, and B2B form simplification—typically deliver a 25% to 50% lift in conversion rates within the first 14 to 30 days.</p>

<p><strong>Does CRO lower Google Ads CPC and CPA?</strong><br>
Yes. Landing Page Experience is one of the three core pillars of Google Ads Quality Score. Improving conversion rates and page speed directly reduces Cost Per Click (CPC) while cutting Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) in half.</p>
