<h2>🎯 How YouTube Ads Billing Works Across Formats</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>In-Feed Video Ads (Formerly TrueView Discovery):</strong> Your video appears at the top of YouTube search results, on the homepage feed, and next to related videos. <strong>You only pay when a viewer actively clicks your thumbnail</strong> to watch your video. Because viewers choose to click, In-Feed Ads generate the highest subscriber conversion rates.</li>
  <li><strong>Skippable In-Stream Ads:</strong> Plays before or during popular videos on your target channels. <strong>The 30-Second Rule:</strong> If a user skips your ad within the first 5 seconds, <strong>you pay $0.00 (free brand impression!)</strong>. You are only billed when a viewer watches at least 30 seconds or clicks your call-to-action link.</li>
  <li><strong>YouTube Shorts Ads:</strong> Native vertical video ads delivered inside the high-velocity Shorts feed, ideal for mobile-first engagement, music hooks, and e-commerce product discovery.</li>
</ol>
