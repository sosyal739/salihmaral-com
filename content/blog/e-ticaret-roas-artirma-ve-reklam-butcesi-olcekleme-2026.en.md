<h2>📉 1. Why ROAS Crashes When You Scale Spend</h2>
<p>Many brand operators achieve an attractive 6.0x ROAS spending $100/day. However, when increasing spend to $500/day, ROAS plummets to 2.2x and bottom-line profit turns negative. Three mechanical issues trigger this cliff:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Audience Saturation:</strong> Platforms quickly exhaust the bottom-of-funnel shoppers and are forced to bid on colder traffic with lower purchasing intent.</li>
  <li><strong>Tracking Signal Degradation:</strong> Browser cookie blocking (iOS 14+, Safari ITP, AdBlockers) causes 25% to 35% of conversion events to disappear from platform dashboards, blinding AI bidding algorithms.</li>
  <li><strong>Channel Over-Reliance:</strong> Spending exclusively on Instagram or Google Search inflates competitive auction clearing costs (CPM).</li>
</ul>

<h2>⚡ 2. Three High-Impact Moves to Boost ROAS</h2>

<h3>A. Server-Side CAPI & Enhanced Conversions</h3>
<p>By routing transactional data through a dedicated cloud server directly to Meta and Google APIs (including hashed email, phone number, and purchase value), signal match quality climbs above 8.5/10. Google and Meta bidding models instantly re-optimize toward your highest LTV shoppers.</p>

<h3>B. Product Margin Segmentation in Performance Max</h3>
<p>Never dump your entire inventory into a single Performance Max asset pool. Segment best-selling hero products with high gross margins into dedicated campaigns, while allocating separate ROAS targets for clearance or lower-margin categories.</p>

<h3>C. Creative Diversity & Anti-Fatigue Sprints</h3>
<p>Meta Advantage+ Shopping demands continuous creative iteration. Introduce founder stories, customer reviews, unboxings, and side-by-side comparison tables weekly to maintain high click-through rates and suppress rising CPMs.</p>
